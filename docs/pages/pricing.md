| | EN | ES |
|---|---|---|
| Thousands separator | comma — `€1,290` | period — `1.290 €` |
| Currency position | before — `€490` | after, with a space — `490 €` |
| Monthly suffix | `/mo` | `/mes` |

Currency is EUR in both languages. A EUR/USD switcher was raised in the brief as a later possibility; it is not in scope.
